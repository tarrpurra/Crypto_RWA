# Product

## Register

product

## Users
AIxRWA is for builders, operators, and reviewers managing guarded real-world-asset allocation on Mantle. They need to understand portfolio state, market data quality, risk gates, and advisory AI reasoning before any execution path is trusted.

## Product Purpose
AIxRWA is an AI-assisted RWA portfolio and risk terminal. It combines live market and chain data, deterministic portfolio and risk controls, advisory allocation logic, and guarded smart-contract execution visibility. Success means users can see what the agent recommends, why it recommends it, which controls approve or block it, and what operational action is safe next.

## Brand Personality
Calm, exacting, and operational. The interface should feel like a serious control room for capital decisions, not a speculative trading toy or generic AI landing page.

## Anti-references
Avoid decorative AI SaaS pages, gradient-heavy crypto dashboards, vague "autopilot" claims, cloned reference layouts, and interfaces that hide risk behind large marketing cards. Do not imply AI can override deterministic policy controls.

## Design Principles
1. Show control before autonomy: every AI recommendation needs visible policy, risk, and data-quality context.
2. Make degraded states useful: stale data, testnet limitations, and missing addresses should be explicit and actionable.
3. Keep capital workflows scannable: prioritize status, exposure, risk, and next action over decorative content.
4. Separate signal from simulation: clearly distinguish live chain state, modeled outcomes, and demo-only assumptions.
5. Preserve operator trust: use restrained motion, precise labels, and consistent hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve reduced-motion behavior for animated elements, avoid color-only status communication, and keep dense dashboard content readable on laptop and mobile viewports.
